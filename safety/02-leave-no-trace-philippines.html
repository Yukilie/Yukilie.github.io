Skills & Safety • Philippines

Leave No Trace Philippines

Plan ahead, stay on marked trails, dispose of waste properly, leave natural objects where they are, respect wildlife, and be considerate of other visitors and communities.

Tips

  • Plan ahead and prepare before visiting parks.
  • Stay on marked trails to protect plants and prevent erosion.
  • Dispose of trash properly and bring your waste home when necessary.
  • Leave rocks, plants, shells, and other natural objects untouched.
  • Respect wildlife and never feed or disturb animals.
  • Avoid damaging trees, plants, and natural surroundings.
  • Respect local traditions, communities, and cultural sites.
  • Keep noise low and be considerate of other visitors.
  • Use designated camping and picnic areas when available.
  • Help keep parks clean by picking up your own litter.
